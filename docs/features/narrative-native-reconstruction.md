# Narrative-Native Generation Reconstruction

## Contract

- **Status:** Verified within the scoped automated reconstruction contract. Human blind recognition and representative real Civ V loading remain separate empirical gates and are not implied by this status.
- **User outcome:** Every Map Type must be recognisable because its owning Generation Engine natively constructs the relationships, processes and gameplay obligations that define it. A finished map must not be a generic engine result cosmetically pushed toward a silhouette.
- **Scope:** All thirty-three owner-engine Map Types; complete semantic programmes; engine-native grammar families; deterministic negotiation and candidate selection; type-specific surface, resource, wonder, start and city-state obligations; causal evidence; protection; history; project persistence; Review; regression, build and Alpine verification.
- **Failure behavior:** Civ V legality, explicit protection, explicit controls, accessibility, hydrology and legal settlement capacity outrank narrative preference. An infeasible identity is rejected or weakened through its authored relaxation order. The generator must never change explicit controls, install an undisclosed hard failure or mutate a completed map through the evaluator.
- **Exclusions:** This phase does not expose every Map Type through every engine, claim scientific world simulation, change Civ V rules, promise mathematical multiplayer balance, restore unverified generated scenario exports or claim human recognisability without Identity Lab evidence.

## Architectural decision

The neutral narrative programme describes relationships and obligations, never tile coordinates. Each owning engine compiles that programme into its own native vocabulary:

1. **Excogitare:** continuous multi-scale fields, anisotropic growth, basin/ridge/corridor splines and shelf ancestry before thresholding.
2. **Eccentric:** parent polygons, graph membership, reserved edges, hierarchical subdivision, regional collections and graph-native hydrology.
3. **Physical:** crust and plate conditions, boundary classes, subsidence, uplift, erosion, circulation, moisture and drainage outlets.
4. **Polis:** strategic nodes, roles, fronts, objectives and route redundancy followed by asymmetric geographic disguise and strategic revalidation.

The shared evaluator extracts semantic evidence after native generation. It only measures and reports; it cannot select a candidate, reject a candidate, authorize a relaxation or write tiles. The deterministic negotiation orchestrator uses that read-only evidence to select, reject or retry a native plan and records any authored relaxation it applies.

## Completion gates

1. **Complete contracts:** Every identity has authored essential relationships, soft preferences, anti-motifs, causal requirements, gameplay/content obligations, scale laws, measurable evidence and a meaningful ordered relaxation plan. A generic stronger copy of the final essential constraint is not a sufficient soft programme.
2. **Native grammar data:** Every identity selects an explicit grammar family and typed parameters. No identity relies solely on inferred role-name effects.
3. **Excogitare behavior:** Its eight identities compile into continuous field sources before topology thresholding. Exact shores remain engine results; retained sources explain each semantic object.
4. **Eccentric behavior:** Its eleven identities reserve graph components and edges before subdivision. Final topology must not cut across completed graph units to manufacture the identity.
5. **Physical behavior:** Its seven identities alter initial or boundary conditions. Ranges, arcs, rain shadows, glaciation and drainage require retained physical causes.
6. **Polis behavior:** Its seven identities retain their strategic contracts while visual symmetry and literal board shapes are independently discouraged. Geographic disguise cannot destroy route or capacity obligations.
7. **Content obligations:** Starts, city states, resources and wonders follow the selected narrative where applicable without violating Civ V legality or the five-hex global start rule. Ruins and barbarian camps continue to obey their explicit abundance, spacing and legality controls; this reconstruction does not claim a per-Map-Type ruin or barbarian grammar unless one is separately specified and tested.
8. **Negotiation:** Standard, Thorough and Exhaustive use deterministic bounded candidate policies. Hard semantic misses reject or invoke the minimum authored relaxation; aggregate scores cannot conceal them.
9. **Protection/editing:** Tile and semantic protection enter the native grammar before completion; Refine, Iterate and history retain or invalidate evidence accurately.
10. **Persistence/export:** `.excogitare` retains the complete programme, native plan, causal objects, evaluation and relaxations. Ordinary `.Civ5Map` remains geography-only.
11. **Interface:** Design remains compact. Review presents independent Legal, Narrative, Engine and Perceptual axes and explains the concrete consequences of every weakening.
12. **Verification:** Exhaustive catalogue tests, hand-built semantic fixtures, deterministic engine tests, Repair-clean game output, project round trips, worker behavior, rendered UI, TypeScript, lint, production, Pages and Node 24 Alpine pass. Identity Lab sessions and Civ V loading are reported separately.
13. **Reconciliation:** The register, this record, detailed plan, code, tests, README and retained baselines agree before any status is advanced.
