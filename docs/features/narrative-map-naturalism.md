# Narrative Map Naturalism

## Contract

- **Status:** Implemented; automated verification complete, with human aesthetic recognition and representative Civ V play remaining empirical.
- **User outcome:** A Narrative Map Type may look deliberately composed, exaggerated or fantastical, but it must not look physically arbitrary. Every visible large-scale form must be explainable by the identity's retained geological, ecological, hydrological or strategic cause rather than by the implementation graph used to construct it.
- **Scope:** All thirty-three owner-engine Map Types; Duel through Standard representative scales; Realistic, Fantastical, Mundane and Brutal character interpretations where compatible; final topology, relief, climate, drainage, starts, content and retained native evidence; deterministic candidate negotiation; Review and audit reporting.
- **Failure behavior:** Naturalism is evaluated after final legality and content normalization. A candidate with a non-relaxable naturalism contradiction is rejected. An authored relaxation may disclose and reduce an identity obligation, but it cannot authorize a final climate/terrain contradiction, a malformed river, a literal one-tile relationship wire, an inaccessible start or a false retained cause. Explicit water, mountain, population, protection and content controls remain authoritative.
- **Exclusions:** Naturalism does not require photographic realism, Earth-like continents, equal realms, low symmetry, subdued fantasy, or a single preferred visual style. Automated morphology is not a substitute for human blind-recognition sessions or Civ V playtesting.

## Decision rule

Artificial-looking geography is acceptable when the visible exaggeration still has an in-world cause. Examples include an enormous rift, a geometrically memorable caldera, a deliberately tripolar political world or an unnaturally scarred alien pangaea. Geography is rejected as unnatural when the implementation mechanism itself becomes the only explanation: ruler-straight full-map walls, rectangular edge frames, repeated equal tokens, one-tile land wires, climate stamps that disagree with retained temperature or moisture, collision belts without collision relief, or tiny local objects offered as proof of a world-scale premise.

## Acceptance gates

1. **Latent construction:** Strategic and semantic graph edges constrain fields, regions and relationships; they are not automatically rasterized as literal visible paths.
2. **Scale coherence:** Required objects have map-size-sensitive area, length, width, hierarchy and coverage. A tiny valid object cannot prove a world-scale identity.
3. **Morphology:** Final maps reject unexplained microcomponents, long one-tile filaments, excessive ruler-straight runs, collapsed sibling systems, repeated equal token geometry and implausibly narrow load-bearing sutures.
4. **Causal surface fidelity:** Final terrain, elevation and features agree with retained relief, temperature, moisture, tectonic and ecological causes. Late content or safety passes cannot silently repaint away that agreement.
5. **Hydrology:** Retained rivers reconstruct into directed Civ V edge systems with credible sources, outlets and map-scale length. Watershed identities additionally require branching and coverage rather than isolated legal stubs.
6. **Climate transitions:** Incompatible biomes require a causal buffer, relief boundary, latitude transition or explicitly fantastical retained cause. Regional effects must be internally composed rather than scattered singletons.
7. **Geographic disguise:** Polis retains its exact strategic graph while final routes and regions follow credible coasts, valleys, passes, basins, shelves and frontiers. Literal rings, spokes, wedges and quadrants are rejected when the terrain provides no independent cause.
8. **Population and starts:** Realm counts adapt to supported player counts, and final starts remain in the intended accessible homes after all native and content passes.
9. **Negotiation and evidence:** Naturalism findings are deterministic, persisted with the selected candidate and stated explicitly in audit output. Retry-seed variation cannot masquerade as an authored improvement.
10. **Verification:** Every owner has representative multi-seed Duel and Standard positive coverage plus adversarial morphology corruption. Tests also cover exact controls, deterministic repetition, Repair, geography-only binary export, TypeScript, lint, production, Pages and Node 24 Alpine. Contact sheets remain review evidence rather than automatic proof.

## Audit baseline — 2026-08-16

The initiating read-only audit inspected 197 generated final maps across all thirty-three types and ran twenty-one additional Brutal Polis compatibility probes. Strict native proof and Repair were substantially greener than the maps' visible causality: all forty-two sampled Physical maps and sixty-five successful Eccentric maps passed both gates, yet collision belts without mountains, climate-inconsistent refuges, repeated parallel island arcs, rectangular inland-sea frames, continuous land wires standing in for broken island chains and other contradictions remained visible. Excogitare also exposed fixed realm packing, single-sine relationship paths and player-count failures; Polis repeatedly exposed its strategic graph as rings, spokes, wedges, narrow bars and equal island tokens.

This feature therefore extends rather than replaces the verified native reconstruction. Existing identity proofs continue to protect the authored premise. Naturalism adds final-state scale, morphology and causal-consistency obligations that those proofs intentionally did not claim.
